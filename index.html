Questions

Answers, mostly.

Can I really make $1,000,000 in one week?

No. This site is a class project and a joke. In real life, most people who try dropshipping earn far less than that, and plenty lose money on ads and returns. Treat any real course that promises a million dollars in a week with serious suspicion.

Because it sounds smaller than $1,400.
